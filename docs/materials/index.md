# Materials guide

<p class="roles">Materials · Warehouse · Quality · Manufacturing</p>

The **Materials** pages are for contractors who buy, stock, build and ship physical things: hardware deliverables,
spares, test equipment, kits. They tie into projects and contracts the same way labor does, so material cost lands on
the right WBS element and government-furnished material is tracked but never valued.

| Area | Pages | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inventory** | Parts, inventory, receiving, transactions, physical inventory, landed cost, inventory reports, materials setup | [Parts and inventory](inventory.md) |
| **Manufacturing** | Bills of materials, work orders, MRP, kits, backflush, capacity schedule | [Manufacturing](manufacturing.md) |
| **Quality & engineering** | Inspection, nonconformances, corrective actions, supplier quality, engineering changes | [Quality and engineering](quality.md) |
| **Sales and shipping** | Sales orders, pick lists, shipments, DD Form 250 | [Sales orders and shipping](shipping.md) |

Warehouse work can be done on a phone or scanner: **Receive on a phone**, **Pick on a phone** and **Count on a phone**
(see [Workbench on your phone](../getting-started/mobile.md#warehouse-pages)).

## Ownership: company, contract and government

Every unit of stock has an **owner**:

- **General** (company) stock can be issued to any project or work order.
- **Contract** inventory, bought for one contract, is issued only to its project.
- **Government-furnished material (GFM)** is issued only to its project and never valued; it's counted for property
  reporting.

## Set up first

**Materials setup** (Materials › Inventory) holds the **warehouses and bins** stock is kept in and the **work centers**
used for manufacturing capacity. Set those up before adding parts.
