# Workbench quick reference: Employee

**Sign in** with your work account. **Help:** select **?** at the top of any page · **Search anything:** `Ctrl+K` (`⌘K` on a Mac)

## Every day

- **Enter your hours** in **Time**, on the day you work them. Every hour, on the task you actually worked on,
  including company work (training, proposals) and leave.
- Check **Home** for your to-do list: what's overdue, due today and this week.

## End of the timesheet period

1. Every working day has hours (holidays and leave on their codes).
2. **Certify and submit** › tick **I certify this timesheet** › **Certify and submit**.
3. "Sent back for changes"? Read the comment, fix, certify again.

## Fixing a mistake

| Timesheet is… | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Change it and **Save**. |
| Submitted | Click the hours › **Recall** with a reason › fix › submit again. |
| Approved | Click the hours › **Reopen for correction** with a reason › fix › submit again. |
| In a closed month | Ask Finance. |

Good reason: "Entered 8 h on 1001.01.02 instead of 1001.01.03". Not: "fix".

## Expenses

**Expenses** › **Start an expense report** › **Add line** per expense, with the charge code · attach receipts (at or
above the company limit, always for lodging) · travel: **Add per diem** › **Save and compute** › **Update expense
lines** · **Submit for approval**.

## Leave

**Leave** › **Request leave** › type, dates, hours › **Send request**. On the day, put the hours on the leave code
(e.g. Paid time off) in your timesheet.

## Something wrong?

Account menu (your name, top right) › **Report a problem / Send feedback**. Follow it under **My support requests**.

## Words you'll see

**Charge code**: the task you record time against · **Indirect**: company work, not for one customer ·
**Unallowable**: can't be billed to the government · **Per diem**: daily travel allowance for lodging and meals ·
**DCAA**: the government auditors who check timesheets. Full list: Help › Glossary.
