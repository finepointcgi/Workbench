# Finance training: one hour

**Audience:** accounting, billing, payables and payroll staff. **Goal:** leave able to work the month-end checklist,
bill an invoice, pay bills in a payment run, reconcile the bank and post the revenue run, and know where the incurred
cost package lives.

**Before the session:** a fresh demo database. Trainees sign in as `pm@example.com` (Pat Morgan has the Finance role).
Hand out the [Finance quick-reference card](card-finance.md). This hour is a tour of the workflows; plan follow-up
sessions per module for the people who own them.

| Time | Topic | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Welcome | Workbench as the system of record: time, ledger, billing and payables in one place. |
| 0:05–0:10 | Getting around | Welcome tour, Finance section, Reports, Ctrl+K, ? help (every Finance page has an article). |
| 0:10–0:22 | Month-end close | The checklist, Post backlog, what each check means, closing and reopening. Exercise F1. |
| 0:22–0:34 | Billing | New invoice, preview lines, funding and ceilings, approve and post, SF 1034 / WAWF, payments. Exercise F2. |
| 0:34–0:42 | Paying bills | Bills, three-way match and exceptions, payment runs, ACH and positive pay. Exercise F3. |
| 0:42–0:50 | Bank reconciliation | Import a statement, auto-match, post and match fees, complete. Exercise F4. |
| 0:50–0:55 | Revenue and ICE | Revenue estimates and the revenue run; the incurred cost package at year-end. |
| 0:55–1:00 | Questions | Reports, financial statements, where to report problems. |

## Key messages

- The close checklist is the definition of done for a month; each failing check links to the fix.
- Hours and costs are billed once; voids and credit memos are the only way to undo an invoice.
- Unallowable costs are recorded and reported but never burdened or billed.
- Every posting goes through the general ledger, and closed periods refuse changes.
