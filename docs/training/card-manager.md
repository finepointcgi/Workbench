# Workbench quick reference: Manager

**Help:** **?** on any page · **Search:** `Ctrl+K` · Your own time, expenses and leave work like everyone else's (see the employee card).

## Approvals

- **Approvals** (menu) holds everything waiting on you; the number shows how many. **Home › Waiting on you** lists the
  oldest; timesheets can be approved right there.
- **Approve** · **Return** (send back to fix, with a comment) · **Reject** (stop it, with a reason).
- **Several at once:** tick the rows (or pick a type, e.g. **Timesheet**, and tick the header box for all of them),
  add one comment if you like, then **Approve** or **Reject** (rejecting needs the comment as the reason). Each item is
  still checked on its own; the summary says which couldn't be done and why.
- **Time on my projects:** hours charged to projects you manage, when your timesheet workflow has a project manager step.
- **From email:** approval emails have **Approve** and **Reject** links. They open Workbench to confirm, work once, only
  for you, and expire after 7 days.
- You can't approve your own items; they go to your supervisor.

**Timesheet checklist:** every working day has hours · hours on the right tasks · corrections have a clear reason.

**Going away?** Approvals › **Delegate** › person and dates.

## Your projects

**Projects** › pick one.

| Tab | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | See health (On track / At risk / Over) and why, spend vs budget, funding used. |
| Plan | Open the plan sheet and Gantt chart. |
| Financials | Cost, revenue and margin by task, direct and burdened. |
| Budgets | Review the budget and your EAC (estimate at completion). |
| Earned value | CPI, SPI and variance reports (EVM projects). |
| Ceilings | Watch hours, cost and billing limits before charging is blocked. |
| Costs | Vendor invoices, travel and other direct costs. |
| Team | Who charged hours, and how many. |

## Every month

1. Approve timesheets and expenses promptly (billing and the month-end close wait on them).
2. Review your EAC when Home says it's due.
3. Explain variances (VARs) by their due date.
4. Act on ceiling and funding warnings.

## Words

**EAC**: expected total cost at the end · **BAC**: total budget · **CPI/SPI**: cost/schedule efficiency (below 1.0 is
behind) · **CLIN**: separately funded contract line · **Funded**: money obligated so far; you can't bill past it.
