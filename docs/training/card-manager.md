# Workbench quick reference: Manager

**Help:** **?** on any page · **Search:** `Ctrl+K` · Your own time, expenses and leave work like everyone else's (see the employee card).

## Approvals

- **Approvals** (menu) holds everything waiting on you; the number shows how many. **Home › Waiting on you** lists the
  oldest; timesheets can be approved right there.
- **Approve** · **Return** (send back to fix, with a comment) · **Reject** (stop it, with a reason).
- **Several at once:** tick the rows (or pick a type, e.g. **Timesheet**, and tick the header box for all of them),
  add one comment if you like, then **Approve** or **Reject** (rejecting needs the comment as the reason). Each item is
  still checked on its own; the summary says which couldn't be done and why.
- **Time on my projects:** only the hours charged to projects you manage (e.g. "12.0 h on 1002 of 40.0 h"), when an
  administrator turns on Setup › Approval workflows › "Project managers approve time charged to their projects".
- **From email:** approval emails have **Approve** and **Reject** links. They open Workbench to confirm, work once, only
  for you, and expire after 7 days.
- You can't approve your own items; they go to your supervisor.

**Timesheet checklist:** every working day has hours · hours on the right tasks · corrections have a clear reason.

**Going away?** Approvals › **Delegate** › person and dates.

## Plan a project (Plan tab)

Finance sets up a project from its contract (ceiling, funding, contract type, fee) and names you its manager: you get
**Plan {project}** on your home page and a notification.

- **The rows are the WBS.** Type a row and press Enter; **Tab** makes it a subtask. Codes number themselves, and the
  lowest rows are the charge codes people charge to (milestones aren't). Once time is charged to a code it keeps its
  code, and its row can't be deleted.
- **People** grid under the plan: one row per person and task with labor category, hours and dates. Type in the last
  row, **Tab** between cells, or paste rows from Excel (person, task, hours, from, to). Problems show on the row.
- **The money bar** at the top: *Planned $X of $Y ceiling · $Z funded · $W left*. Amber is past funding, red is past
  the ceiling, for the project and each CLIN.
- **⚠ Over their hours** on a person: **Fix** spreads their hours over more weeks, moves the task after their other
  work, or gives the hours to someone in the same labor category. Nothing levels the plan by itself.
- **Try changes**: move a task or change people, see the new finish, cost, ceiling and overbookings, then **Apply
  changes** or **Discard**. **Undo** in the bar puts back the last change.
- **Submit plan for approval** when it's ready. Finance approves it (it becomes the baseline budget) or returns it with
  comments. After approval, changes show against the baseline until you submit them and Finance approves them too.

## Every month

1. Approve timesheets and expenses promptly (billing and the month-end close wait on them).
2. Review your EAC when Home says it's due.
3. Explain variances (VARs) by their due date.
4. Act on ceiling and funding warnings.

## Words

**EAC**: expected total cost at the end · **BAC**: total budget · **CPI/SPI**: cost/schedule efficiency (below 1.0 is
behind) · **CLIN**: separately funded contract line · **Funded**: money obligated so far; you can't bill past it.
