# Employee training: one hour

**Audience:** everyone who records time. **Goal:** leave able to enter time daily, submit a timesheet, correct a past
day, file an expense report and request leave, and know where to get help.

**Before the session:** a fresh demo database; each trainee signed in as `analyst@example.com` (Alex Rivera), or
`viewer@example.com` (Val Chen) for part-time staff. Hand out the [employee quick-reference card](card-employee.md).

| Time | Topic | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Welcome | Why we're moving to Workbench; one place for time, expenses and leave. |
| 0:05–0:12 | Getting around | The welcome tour, Home (the to-do list), the menu, Ctrl+K search, the ? help button. |
| 0:12–0:27 | Time, the DCAA way | The five rules (daily, every hour, only what you did, changes leave a trail, you certify). Exercises E1–E3. |
| 0:27–0:37 | Correcting time | Recall vs reopen, writing a good reason. Exercise E4. |
| 0:37–0:47 | Expenses and per diem | Lines, receipts, unallowable items, per diem. Exercise E5. |
| 0:47–0:53 | Leave | Balances, requesting, recording leave on the timesheet. Exercise E6. |
| 0:53–1:00 | Help and questions | Help center, glossary, Report a problem (Exercise E7), questions. |

## Key messages

- Enter time **every day**. It is the single most important habit, and it's what auditors check first.
- If a task isn't in your list, ask your project manager; never charge time "somewhere close".
- A correction with a reason is normal and fine. A silent change is not possible, by design.
- Receipts are needed at or above the company limit and always for lodging; per diem meals need none.
- The **?** button answers most questions about the page you're on.

## Common questions

- *I forgot to enter yesterday.* Enter it now; Workbench notes it was entered late. Tell your supervisor if it's often.
- *My timesheet says "Sent back for changes".* Read the comment in the banner, fix it and certify again.
- *Where's my pay stub?* Not in Workbench; payroll stays with the payroll provider.
