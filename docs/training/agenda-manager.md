# Manager training: one hour

**Audience:** supervisors and project managers. **Goal:** leave able to clear the approvals inbox, review a timesheet
properly, read the project page and act on its warnings, and delegate approvals before time off.

**Before the session:** a fresh demo database. Trainees sign in as `pm@example.com` (Pat Morgan). Have one trainer
signed in as `analyst@example.com` to submit things for the class to approve. Hand out the
[manager quick-reference card](card-manager.md).

| Time | Topic | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Welcome | What changes for managers: one inbox, one page per project. |
| 0:05–0:10 | Getting around | Welcome tour, Home ("Waiting on you"), Approvals, Projects, Ctrl+K, ? help. |
| 0:10–0:25 | Approving | What to check on a timesheet, approve vs reject vs return, approving from Home. Exercises M1–M2. |
| 0:25–0:30 | Delegation | Setting a delegate for time off. Exercise M3. |
| 0:30–0:50 | The project page | Overview and health, Plan (Gantt), Financials, Budgets and EAC, Ceilings, Costs, Team. Exercises M4–M5. |
| 0:50–0:55 | Monthly rhythm | EAC review, variance explanations, ceilings warnings; all appear on Home when due. |
| 0:55–1:00 | Help and questions | Help center articles for managers; questions. |

## Key messages

- Approve on time: late approvals hold up billing and the month-end close.
- **Return** when something needs fixing, **Reject** only when it shouldn't happen at all, and always say why.
- You can't approve your own time or expenses; they go to your own supervisor.
- Health "At risk" is an early warning, not a failure: look at the reason on the Overview tab.
- Ceilings warn before they block. Act on the warning.
