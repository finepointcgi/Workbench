# Workbench quick reference: Finance

**Help:** **?** on any page (every Finance page has an article) · **Search:** `Ctrl+K` · Close steps that are due appear on **Home**.

## Month-end close: Finance › Accounting › Period close

Pick the period › **Post backlog** › work each failing check (each links to its fix) › **Close {period}**.
Checks include: time and ODCs decided · everything posted · payables · payroll · depreciation · indirect allocation ·
revenue run · unbilled accrual · bank reconciliation · trial balance balances. **Reopen period** needs a reason.

## New projects and plan approval

**Projects › New project** › **A contract** › pick it: its ceiling, funding, type, fee and dates fill in (set this
project's share when the contract has other projects) › pick the **Project manager** › **Create**. The PM gets a to-do
to plan it. When they submit, **Review the plan for {project}** is on your home page: on the Plan tab, **Approve** (the
plan becomes the locked Baseline and the Current working budget) or type comments and **Return to PM**. Later changes
show against the baseline until they're submitted and approved. **Change the ceiling or funding** is on the Plan tab.

## Billing: Finance › Billing & AR

**New invoice** › contract and period › **Preview lines** › check funding and ceilings › **Approve and post** ›
**SF 1034** or **WAWF CSV** › **Mark sent**. Payments: **Customer payments › Record payment**. Fix a posted invoice with
**Void** or **Credit memo**. **AR aging › Send overdue reminders**.

## Payables: Finance › Payables & purchasing

**Bills › New bill** (or **Create bill from PO**) › **Submit** › approvals · mismatches land in **Match exceptions**
(fix or **Override** with a reason) · **Payment runs › Create payment run** › **ACH CSV** / **Positive pay CSV** ›
**Record payments and post**.

## Cash: Finance › Cash

**Import statements** › **Reconcile** › **Auto-match** › match the rest · **Post and match** for fees and interest ›
**Complete** at zero difference.

## Revenue: Finance › Revenue & compliance › Revenue

PMs submit estimates; you approve them in **Revenue estimates** · **Record delivery** for milestone projects · review
the period › **Post revenue run** (**Reverse** to redo).

## Incurred cost (yearly): Revenue & compliance › Incurred cost

**New package** › **Generate draft** › **Save inputs** › **Mark reviewed** › officer **Certify** › **Export .xlsx** ›
**Mark submitted**. All months closed and rates final first.

## Rules to remember

Hours and costs bill once · unallowable cost is never burdened or billed · closed periods refuse postings · every change
to rates and setup is in the audit log.
