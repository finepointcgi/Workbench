# DCAA accounting system readiness: SF 1408 self-assessment

Before awarding a cost-type contract (or a T&M/LH contract with cost-reimbursable parts), the contracting officer must
find the contractor's accounting system **adequate** (FAR 16.301-3, 16.104(i)). DCAA tests it with a pre-award survey
documented on **Standard Form 1408**, *Pre-award Survey of Prospective Contractor: Accounting System*, and, for
contracts with DFARS 252.242-7006, against the 18 accounting system criteria in paragraph (c) of that clause
(SF 1408 items 1–12 correspond to criteria (c)(2)–(c)(5) and (c)(9)–(c)(17)).

This document maps every SF 1408 criterion (and the DFARS expectations DCAA usually tests alongside) to the Workbench
feature that meets it and the **report that proves it**, and flags what a **GovCon CPA must review**. The same
checklist is in the app: **Admin › Compliance › DCAA readiness** (`/admin/dcaa-readiness`, administrators), with live
checks of your data and a place to record each answer (kept with who and when, as evidence).

> Workbench gives you an accounting system designed to these criteria. Adequacy is a finding about *your* system as
> operated: your written policies, your chart of accounts and indirect rate structure, your people following them.
> Have a CPA experienced with government contracts review it before a survey.

## How to use it

1. Set up Workbench: chart of accounts with cost classes, indirect pools and bases, contracts with CLINs, projects and
   WBS (charge codes marked Direct, Indirect or Leave), employees and cost rates, timesheet period, approval workflows.
2. Run at least one full month: time entered daily and approved, ODCs and AP bills posted, payroll posted, allocations
   run, the period closed, an invoice generated.
3. Open **DCAA readiness**. Each item shows a live check (✓ / ⚠ / ℹ). Review the evidence report, then record your
   assessment (Meets, Partially meets, Doesn't meet, Not applicable) with notes.
4. Give the CPA the items marked **CPA review** and this document; record their conclusions in the notes.
5. Write the policies in section C. DCAA asks for them first.

## A. SF 1408 criteria

| # | SF 1408 criterion | Workbench feature | Report that proves it | CPA review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Proper segregation of direct costs from indirect costs** | Chart of accounts cost classes (direct labor, direct ODC, indirect labor, fringe, overhead, G&A, unallowable); every WBS charge code is Direct, Indirect or Leave; posting rules route each kind of cost to its account | Chart of accounts (Finance › Accounts); trial balance by cost class (Financial statements); Projects and WBS (charge kinds) | — |
| 2 | **Identification and accumulation of direct costs by contract** | Contract → project → WBS charge codes; labor (approved hours × effective cost rate), ODCs, expenses, AP bills and subcontracts post to the charged project/WBS | **Project status report** (Reports › Standard › Project status); project financials; labor distribution | — |
| 3 | **Logical and consistent method for allocating indirect costs** to intermediate and final cost objectives | Indirect pools and bases with the fringe → overhead → G&A cascade; provisional, target and actual rates; allocation runs post applied burden through the GL | **Indirect rate variance** report; Indirect rates page; allocation journal entries | **Yes**: pool/base structure, cascade, consistency with your disclosure (CAS 401/402/418, CAS 410 if covered), variance settlement |
| 4 | **Accumulation of costs under general ledger control** | Every subledger posts to the GL through one journal service, idempotently per source document; posted entries immutable, corrected by reversal; project cost reconciles to the GL | Trial balance; journal; project cost to GL reconciliation view | — |
| 5 | **Timekeeping system that identifies employees' labor by cost objective** | Daily entry on open, chargeable leaf charge codes only (assignment rules); employee certification on submit; supervisor approval, no self-approval; revisions after submission kept with a reason; missing-time reminders; closed periods locked | **Timesheet compliance** report (missing/late timesheets, late entries, revisions); timesheet history; audit log | **Yes**: written timekeeping policy, training and acknowledgements, floor-check readiness |
| 6 | **Labor distribution system** charging direct and indirect labor to the right cost objectives | Labor cost always computed from approved hours × the cost rate effective on the work date, to direct or indirect charge codes; payroll register reconciled to labor distribution | **Labor distribution** report; payroll reconciliation | — |
| 7 | **Interim (at least monthly) determination of costs** through routine posting of books | Monthly fiscal periods; period close checklist (time/ODCs posted, accruals, depreciation, bank reconciliation, allocations); closing locks the period | Period close page; monthly financial statements; project status by period | — |
| 8 | **Exclusion of unallowable costs** (FAR Part 31, contract terms) | Unallowable flag with a required reason on ODCs and expense lines; unallowable GL accounts; unallowable cost never burdened or billed; incurred cost schedules exclude it | Incurred cost (ICE) schedules; unallowable account ledger; ODC list filtered to unallowable | **Yes**: expressly unallowable cost policy, mapping of accounts to FAR 31.205, penalty exposure (FAR 42.709) |
| 9 | **Costs by contract line item and units** (if the contract requires) | CLINs/SLINs with WBS mapped to them; cost, funding and billing by CLIN | **Contract funding and ceiling status** report | — (mark N/A if not required) |
| 10 | **Segregation of preproduction from production costs** (if applicable) | No dedicated feature: use separate projects/WBS | Project cost by WBS | **Yes**: usually N/A for services; record why |
| 11 | **Financial information for Limitation of Cost/Funds (FAR 52.232-20/22) or Limitation on Payments (52.216-16), and progress payments** | Funded value per CLIN through funding actions; Limitation of Funds notices at 75% and 90%; ceilings (warn/block); commitments. Progress payment requests (SF 1443) are **not** generated | Contract funding and ceiling status; funding notices; commitments | **Yes**: SF 1443 preparation procedure if progress payments apply |
| 12 | **Adequate data for pricing follow-on acquisitions** | Actuals by labor category, employee and WBS; rate history; indirect rate history; earned value; report writer | Labor distribution; indirect rate variance; report writer exports | **Yes**: link to estimating practices (and DFARS 252.215-7002 if it applies) |
| 13 | **Is the system in full operation?** | All modules above run in Workbench; the live check looks for recent posting | Journal (recent posted entries); period close history | **Yes**: state which modules are live and since when |

## B. Also tested (DFARS 252.242-7006 and DCAA practice)

| Area | DFARS 252.242-7006(c) | Workbench feature | Evidence | CPA review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Billing** from the books: cost-type invoices at provisional billing rates, no unallowables, nothing billed twice | (16) | Invoices generated from approved time, ODCs and milestones; each source billed once until voided; provisional rates; rate and fee caps; invoices post to GL and AR | Invoices; billing accrual; AR aging | **Yes**: provisional billing rates, cumulative billing method, direct-billing eligibility |
| **Period close**, reconciliation and adjusting entries | (6), (7), (11) | Closed periods reject new time, ODCs and journal entries; corrections are reversing entries; `period.status_changed` events | Period close page; security events | — |

## C. Policies to write (a CPA should draft or review them)

| Policy | Covers | Points at in Workbench |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounting manual | Chart of accounts, cost classes, cost accumulation, period close calendar | Accounts, posting rules, period close |
| Timekeeping policy | Daily recording, charging the work actually performed, corrections with reasons, supervisor approval, training, consequences | Time entry, approvals, timesheet compliance report |
| Unallowable cost policy | FAR 31.205 categories, how they're recorded and excluded | Unallowable accounts, ODC unallowable flag |
| Indirect cost policy (disclosure) | Pools, bases, cascade, rate calculation, provisional rates, true-up | Indirect rates |
| Billing policy | Invoice preparation, provisional rates, review and approval | Billing |
| Labor charging for indirect work | B&P, IR&D, overhead charge codes | Charge kinds |
| Records retention | FAR 4.703 | Retention (protected records), legal holds |

## D. Evidence package for the survey

1. This checklist exported from the app (screenshot or print) with notes and the CPA's sign-off.
2. Trial balance and chart of accounts with cost classes.
3. Project status report for a sample month; labor distribution; timesheet compliance report.
4. Indirect rate variance report; indirect rate structure description.
5. A sample invoice with its supporting detail.
6. Period close record for the last three months.
7. The policies in section C.

Contractors pamphlet) are the authority; check the current versions.*
