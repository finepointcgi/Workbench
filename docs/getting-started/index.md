# Getting started

Everything you need for your first day in Workbench. It takes about ten minutes to read.

1. [Signing in and your home page](signing-in.md): how to sign in, the welcome tour, and the to-do list that greets you.
2. [Finding your way around](getting-around.md): the menu, **+ New**, search (++ctrl+k++), the company switcher and dark mode.
3. [Help, notifications and feedback](help.md): the **?** help panel, the help center and glossary, notifications,
   and how to report a problem.
4. [Workbench on your phone](mobile.md): the mobile view for time, receipts, approvals and warehouse work.

Then go to the part of the [user guide](../user-guide/index.md) for what you do every day. Most people start with
[timesheets](../user-guide/time.md).

!!! info "A few words you'll see everywhere"
    - **Charge code**: the project task you record hours and costs against, shown as the task name and its code, for
      example *Requirements (1001.01.02)*.
    - **WBS** (work breakdown structure): a project's outline of tasks. Its lowest-level tasks are the charge codes.
    - **ODC** (other direct cost): a non-labor cost charged to a project, such as travel or a vendor invoice.
    - **Unallowable**: a cost the government won't pay for. It's recorded, but never billed.

    Words like these have a dotted underline in the app: hover over one for a plain-English explanation. The full list
    is in the [glossary](../reference/glossary.md).
