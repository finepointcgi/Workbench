## Who it's for

- **Professional services and consultancies**: time to invoice without re-keying, utilization, rates and margin by project.
- **Engineering and program-managed firms**: WBS, plans, earned value and risks next to the hours that fund them.
- **Government contractors**: DCAA-compliant timekeeping, indirect rates, incurred cost, CMMC assessment and SPRS score.
  Everything defense firms need, without making everyone else look at it.

## FAQ

**Is it really free?** Core is free to self-host with no seat limit, for one company. You pay when you add modules or Seats, or
if you want us to host it.

**What does "source-available" mean for me?** You can read, change and run the code for your own use. The one thing you may not do
is sell a competing service. Each release becomes Apache 2.0 after two years.

**Can we leave?** Yes. Importers and exports are free in every plan, and when a license lapses you fall back to Core with
nothing deleted.

**Do you host it?** `[⚠ Yes, in {regions}, from {date} / Not yet.]` Self-hosting is supported with PostgreSQL.

**Is it secure? Do you have SOC 2?** `[⚠ Only say yes once true.]` Today: audit log, single sign-on, SCIM, MFA, access reviews and
encrypted backups exist in the product. A SOC 2 report is `[planned / in progress / complete]`. See the [SOC 2 readiness](soc2-readiness.md) page.

**Are you a government contractor tool only?** No. The government pack is optional. Most firms never turn it on.

**Can it replace Costpoint, Deltek, Unanet, Smartsheet?** It covers time, projects, billing, the ledger and the sheets that fill
the gaps. Say which parts you need before you decide; there is a migration guide and importers.

**What about CUI and CMMC?** Self-host for controlled data. The hosted service `[⚠ does / does not]` accept it. Workbench helps you
prepare your own CMMC assessment; it does not certify you.

**Do you take payments online?** `[⚠ Not yet: invoiced annually.]`

**Where can I see it?** The public demo `[URL]` and the [guide](../index.md).
