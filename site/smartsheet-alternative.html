A Smartsheet alternative that also does the accounting

If your contract data lives in Smartsheet and your money lives somewhere else, Workbench puts them together: sheets with formulas and dashboards, next to timesheets, billing and the general ledger.

Try the live demoSee pricing

What carries over from Smartsheet

  • Grid with many column types and about 80 formula functions, including cross-sheet references, lookups and SUMIFS.
  • Grid, board, calendar and Gantt views, with filters, sorts and saved views.
  • Dashboards with 17 kinds of widget, and sheet reports.
  • Public forms, sheet automations (alerts, approvals, field changes and webhooks), templates, comments with @mentions and attachments.
  • Sheet permissions, and an importer that brings your Smartsheet sheets across.

What you gain

Because the sheets live in the same system as the books, a project plan can show real hours and real cost, a dashboard can show funding left, and a contract can show what has been invoiced, without exporting and pasting. Time entered on a phone lands on the same project the schedule belongs to.

Where Smartsheet is ahead today

  • Smartsheet has features Workbench does not: update requests, proofing, publishing a sheet as a read-only link, guest collaborators, scheduled imports and exports, and packaging sheets as an app.
  • Smartsheet has a much wider catalogue of ready-made integrations, and mobile apps in the app stores. Workbench has a phone web app you install from the browser.
  • If you only need sheets and no accounting, Smartsheet may be all you need.

Questions

Can I import my Smartsheet sheets?

Yes. There is a Smartsheet importer. Try it on a few sheets first and check the formulas and views carried over the way you expect.

Does it have dashboards and forms?

Yes. Dashboards, reports, public forms and automations are all included, and are free in the Community edition.

What does it cost?

Community is free to self-host. Business is $10 per person per month.

Price

Community is free to run on your own server, for one company, with unlimited people. Business is $10 per person per month and adds more than one company, single sign-on, integrations, customer links, the supplier portal, custom approval workflows and incurred cost submission.
