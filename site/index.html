What we will and will not claim

You are trusting this with your books, so here is where it stands.

Early software, plainly described

  • Workbench is new. It is not yet in use at a customer, and we are looking for a few contractors to try it with real data.
  • The readiness pages help you prepare for an audit. They do not certify that your company is compliant.
  • Some things are not built yet: electronic invoice submission through WAWF/iRAPT, Service Contract Act and Davis-Bacon wage determinations, resource leveling and subprojects. The list is public in our feature comparison.
  • It is English only, and has no offline mode.

Get going

  1. Try the demo. It is filled with sample data. Sign in as an administrator or an employee and click around.
  2. Run it yourself. It runs on your own server with PostgreSQL. Import a copy of your data and see if it adds up.
  3. Add Business when you need it. Paste a licence key into Setup, License. Nothing is deleted if you stop; you drop back to Community.

Questions

What happens if I stop paying?

You drop back to Community after a 14-day grace period. Nothing is deleted, and you can still read everything. Only changing the Business features stops.

Does the licence check call home?

No. The key is verified on your own server, so a self-hosted install never contacts us and works inside a closed network.

Who owns my data?

You do. It sits in a PostgreSQL database you control, and there are importers and export tools so you can leave.

Can I use it with my existing payroll?

There is a payroll provider integration, starting with Paylocity. Tell us what you use.

Is it secure enough for contract data?

It has roles, an audit log, single sign-on and SCIM. A third-party security review has not been done yet, and we will say so until it has.

Can you host it for us?

Yes, on the Business plan, with a separate database for each customer.
