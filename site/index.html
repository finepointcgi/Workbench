Who it's for

Any firm whose product is its people's time. Three ways firms use it. You only turn on what you need, so a consultancy never has to look at government accounting screens.

Consulting and professional services

From hours to invoice, without re-keying

People enter time on a phone or a browser. Approved hours go to a time-and-materials invoice and to the ledger, so the number the project manager sees is the number the client is billed.

  • Timesheets, expenses and leave with approvals
  • Hours, cost and billing by project
  • Billing and the general ledger in the same system
Engineering and construction

Design phases, plans and budgets next to the hours

Break a job into phases and tasks, schedule them, staff them and watch hours and spend against the budget. The plan and the timesheets share one set of project records.

  • A Plan with Gantt, all four dependency types, critical path and baselines
  • Staffing and budget by task
  • Earned value reporting
Government contractors

Books and timekeeping built for the audit

Daily timesheets with certification and approval, indirect rates, ceilings and funding, and readiness pages for DCAA, CAS and CPSR. These stay in their own pack, so they appear only if you are a contractor.

  • Billing types including cost-plus and SF 1443, with PIEE (WAWF) export
  • Incurred cost submission package
  • NIST SP 800-171 readiness documents

Retainers, milestones and recurring billing are in the Billing and revenue module. Ask us whether your billing pattern is covered.

Fewer tools, one login

Most firms keep their numbers in one system and their plans in three others. Workbench puts them next to each other, so a number in a schedule and the same number in an invoice are the same number.

Simple is the point

Business software is usually built for the person who configures it. This is built for the person who has to use it on a Friday afternoon.

Open the phone app, fill in the week, press one button. Your approver gets it, and the hours land on the right project without anyone retyping them. That is the whole job for most of your people.

Most people only enter time

Employees who just record time, expenses and leave need nothing but a phone. They never see a menu of things they cannot use.

One place to look

A global search and a short menu for what your role can do. If you are not allowed to see something, you do not see the button.

Works on a phone

An installable phone app for timesheets and expenses, so nobody waits until Monday to remember their hours.

Bring your old data

Importers for Costpoint, Smartsheet and Unanet, so you are not starting from an empty screen.

Questions

Can I host it myself, or will you host it?

Both. Self-hosting is free for Core and runs on your own server with PostgreSQL. Hosting by us is available on request, with a separate database for each customer. Hosted terms, regions and service levels are still being written, so ask us for the current position.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. It sits in a PostgreSQL database you control, and there are importers and export tools. The importers, file exports and the audit log are free forever, so a lapsed licence never locks you out of your records.

Does it work in an air-gapped or closed network?

Yes. A licence key is verified on your own server against a public key built into the product, so a self-hosted install never contacts us. A key changes only when you enter a new one. A connected licence option is on our list but is not required.

Does it support single sign-on?

Yes, with Microsoft Entra ID and Okta, and SCIM provisioning. Single sign-on is part of Business today and of the Enterprise module.

How secure is it?

It has roles, an audit log, single sign-on and SCIM. We keep a written information security policy set modelled on ISO/IEC 27001, with a risk register and a mapping of controls, and you can read it. It is not a certification: there is no ISO 27001 or SOC 2 report, and a third-party security review has not been done yet. We will say so until it has.

Is it only for government contractors?

No. The government contracting pack is optional, and a firm that does not turn it on does not see it.

Can I use it with my existing payroll?

There is a payroll provider integration, starting with Paylocity. Tell us what you use.

What does source-available mean for me?

You can read, change and run the code for your own use. The one thing you may not do is offer a competing product or service. Each release becomes Apache 2.0 two years after it ships. See the licence.
