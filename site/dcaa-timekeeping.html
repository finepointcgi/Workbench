Timekeeping built for the questions an auditor asks

Workbench records time the way auditors expect to see it: entered daily, certified by the employee, approved by a supervisor and never changed without a trace. It is on every plan, including the free one.

What it records

  • Daily entry. People enter hours as they work, from a browser or the phone app, and the system records when each day was first entered.
  • Certification and approval. The employee certifies the timesheet, and a supervisor approves it.
  • An audit trail. Changes to time, rates and roles are logged with who made them and when.
  • A charge guard. Charging hours to a closed project or contract, or past a funding ceiling you have set to block, is refused.
  • Indirect rates. Rate setup, allocation and an incurred cost submission package.

Readiness pages

Pages for DCAA, CAS and CPSR readiness show where you stand against the questions an auditor asks, so you can find gaps before an audit does. They help you prepare. They do not certify your company.

What to check before you rely on it

  • Have your accountant or compliance lead review the setup against your own policies.
  • Workbench is new and has not been through a DCAA audit at a customer.
  • A third-party security review has not been done yet.

Questions

Is Workbench DCAA compliant?

No software makes a company compliant. Compliance depends on your own policies, how your people use the system and how it is documented. Workbench is built to support the practices auditors look for, and its readiness pages help you check yourself.

Is the audit log in the free edition?

Yes. The audit log, timekeeping and the readiness pages are free on every plan, so you can always show an auditor your records.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. There is an installable phone app for timesheets, expenses, leave and approvals.

Price

Community is free to run on your own server, for one company, with unlimited people. Business is $10 per person per month and adds more than one company, single sign-on, integrations, customer links, the supplier portal, custom approval workflows and incurred cost submission.
