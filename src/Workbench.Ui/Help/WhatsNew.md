title: What's new in Workbench
---
## 2026.10.3 · October 18, 2026

- **Help inside the app.** Select **?** at the top of any page for help about that page, or search the [help center](help).
- **A short welcome tour** the first time you sign in, shaped to your role. You can take it again from the help panel.
- **Report a problem or send feedback** from your account menu. Your administrators see it and you'll hear back in Workbench.
- **A glossary** of government contracting and accounting terms: [open it](help/glossary).

## 2026.10.2 · October 16, 2026

- **A to-do home page** that lists what's overdue, due today and due this week.
- **A simpler menu** that shows only what your role uses, and **Ctrl+K** search for any page, project, sheet or dashboard.
- **Projects hub**: one page per project with its health, plan, money, team and costs.
- Plainer words across the app, with explanations for terms like [EAC](term:eac) and [CLIN](term:clin).

## 2026.10.1 · October 1, 2026

- Ceilings and closeout, earned value, incurred cost submissions and the report writer.
- Payroll with Paylocity, leave balances, per diem and resource planning.
