Workbench is one place for your time, expenses, leave, projects and (if you work in Finance) the books. This page shows you where things are.

## Your home page

**Home** is a to-do list built for you. It shows what's overdue, what's due today and this week: a timesheet to submit, an expense report missing receipts, something waiting for your approval. Each item has a button that takes you straight to it. When the list is empty you'll see "You're all caught up".

Below the list, **This week** shows the hours you've entered against your standard hours, your timesheet status and your leave balances.

## The menu

The menu on the left only shows what you use:

- **My work**: Time, Expenses, Leave and your purchase requests.
- **Approvals**: one inbox for everything waiting on you. It only appears if you approve things.
- **Projects**: the projects you manage or charge to.
- **Sheets & dashboards**: plans, trackers and charts your team shares.
- **Finance**, **Reports** and **Admin**: for people whose role needs them.

On a small screen the menu is hidden; open it with the menu button at the top left.

## Search (Ctrl+K)

Press `Ctrl+K` (`⌘K` on a Mac), or select **Search** at the top, and start typing. You can jump to any page you're allowed to open, a project, a sheet, a dashboard, or a help article. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click a result.

## Getting help

- Select **?** at the top of any page to read help about that page.
- The [help center](help) has every article and the [glossary](help/glossary) explains terms like [EAC](term:eac) and [CLIN](term:clin).
- Something wrong? Choose **Report a problem** from your account menu (your name at the top right). See [Getting help](help:getting-help).

> [!TIP]
> Words with a dotted underline have a plain-English explanation. Hover over them, or move to them with the Tab key.
