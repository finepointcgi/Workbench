Everything waiting on you is in one place: **Approvals**. The number beside it in the menu is how many items are waiting. Your home page also lists the oldest ones under **Waiting on you**.

## Approve or reject

1. Open **Approvals** and select an item to see its details: hours by day, expense lines and receipts, or what's being bought.
2. Select **Approve**, **Reject** or **Return**.
   - **Reject** ends the request, with a reason the person sees.
   - **Return** sends it back for changes, so they can fix it and resubmit.

Timesheets can be approved straight from your home page with the **Approve** button.

## What to check on a timesheet

- Every working day has hours (including leave and holidays).
- Hours are on the right tasks. You know what your people worked on; if something looks wrong, return it.
- Corrections have a clear reason.

You can't approve your own timesheet or expenses; those go to your own supervisor.

## Going away? Delegate

In **Approvals**, select **Delegate** and choose a colleague and the dates. While it's active they can act for you, and the record shows they approved on your behalf.
