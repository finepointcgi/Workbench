## Find an answer

- Select **?** at the top of any page for help about that page.
- Search the [help center](help), or press `Ctrl+K` and type your question.
- The [glossary](help/glossary) explains government contracting and accounting terms.
- **What's new** (in the help panel) lists recent changes to Workbench.

## Report a problem or send feedback

1. Open your account menu (your name at the top right) and choose **Report a problem**. If you see an error message, use its **Report this** button instead.
2. Say what happened in a sentence, and add details if they help: what you were trying to do and what you expected.
3. Optionally attach a screenshot (an image up to 10 MB).
4. Select **Send**.

Workbench adds the page you were on and the version of Workbench, so you don't have to. It never sends what you typed into the page's forms, passwords or anything else.

Your administrators see your report, and it's also sent to your support team. You'll get a notification when its status changes.

## Follow up

Your reports and their status are under **My support requests** (in your account menu). Quote the reference number, for example SR-000042, if you contact support about it.

> [!NOTE]
> Questions about your hours, approvals or pay are best asked of your supervisor or Finance team directly.
