Every year, within six months of year-end, contractors with cost-type contracts send the government their actual costs and indirect rates: the [incurred cost submission](term:incurred-cost).

1. Open **Finance › Revenue & compliance › Incurred cost** and select **New package** for the fiscal year.
2. Select **Generate draft**. Workbench builds the schedules from the general ledger, indirect cost pools, billing and payables (Schedule H, I, and the rest).
3. Fill in the inputs it can't know (for example executive compensation details) and select **Save inputs**. **Regenerate** after any correction in the books.
4. Review each schedule. Select **Mark reviewed** when it's been checked by someone other than the preparer.
5. An officer of the company selects **Certify**. Workbench records who certified and when.
6. Select **Export .xlsx** (or **Print view**), submit it to [DCAA](term:dcaa) and select **Mark submitted**.

> [!WARNING]
> Make sure every month of the year is closed and indirect rates are final before you certify.
