Reconcile each bank account every month, before you close it.

1. Open **Finance › Cash › Import statements**, pick the account and the statement file (CSV or OFX from your bank), and select **Import**.
2. Open **Reconcile** and pick the account and statement.
3. Select **Auto-match**. Workbench pairs statement lines with payments, deposits and journal entries by amount and date.
4. Match what's left by hand. Select a statement line and the book entry it belongs to. **Unmatch** undoes a wrong pair.
5. For bank fees or interest that aren't in the books yet, select **Post and match** to post the entry and match it in one step.
6. When the difference is zero, select **Complete**.

A completed reconciliation is kept in **Reconciliation history** and ticks off the month-end check. To change one, **Reopen** it.
