Mistakes happen. Fixing one the right way keeps your timesheet audit-ready.

## Before you submit

Just change the hours and select **Save**. No reason is needed while the timesheet is still a draft.

## After you've submitted, before it's approved

1. Open the timesheet (use **History** to find an older one).
2. Click into the hours you want to change. Workbench asks you to **Recall** the timesheet.
3. Give a short reason, for example "Charged Tuesday to the wrong task".
4. Make the change, **Save**, then **Certify and submit** again.

## After it's approved

1. Open the timesheet and click into the day to change.
2. Workbench explains the timesheet is approved and locked. Select **Reopen for correction** and give a reason.
3. Make the change and save. The reason is offered again so you don't have to retype it.
4. **Certify and submit** again. Your supervisor approves the corrected version.

Each correction starts a new revision. The old hours, the new hours, who changed them, when and why are all recorded.

> [!WARNING]
> If the month has been closed by Finance, the timesheet can't be changed. Ask Finance; they'll make the correction in an open month.

## Good reasons look like

- "Entered 8 h on 1001.01.02 instead of 1001.01.03"
- "Forgot to record Friday's holiday"
- "Was out sick Monday, not working"

Avoid vague reasons such as "update" or "fix".
