## Check your balance

Open **Leave** to see each balance (for example Paid time off) and how it changed. Your home page shows the same balances under **This week**.

## Ask for time off

1. In **Leave**, select **Request leave**.
2. Pick the type of leave, the dates and the hours per day, and add a note if you like.
3. Select **Send request**. Your approver is notified and you'll hear back in Workbench.

## Record it on your timesheet

Approved leave still goes on your timesheet: on the day, enter the hours on the leave [charge code](term:charge-code) (for example "Paid time off"). That's what takes the hours off your balance. Holidays go on the "Holiday" code and don't use a balance.

> [!TIP]
> Planning a long trip? Request it early so your project manager can plan around it. Approved leave reduces your available hours in resource planning.
