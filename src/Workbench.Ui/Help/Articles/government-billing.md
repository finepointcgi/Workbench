## Set up the contract

Open **Finance › Billing & AR › Contracts and milestones**, pick the contract, and fill in **Government billing**:

- **Invoice format**: the SF 1034 public voucher (long invoices continue on SF 1035 sheets) or your own layout.
- **Contract financing**: none, progress payments, or performance-based payments.
- **PIEE routing**: the issue-by, admin-by and pay office DoDAACs (and ship-to for a Combo), and how delivery invoices are sent.

## Progress payments (SF 1443)

1. Turn on progress payments with the rate (usually 80%, or 90% for a small business) and the liquidation rate.
2. Select **SF 1443 requests**, choose the date costs run through, and select **Preview SF 1443**. Costs come from approved time and costs on the contract's projects; with paid costs only, what is still owed to vendors is left out.
3. Change a figure only if you have better information (for example the cost to complete), then select **Create request**.
4. Approve the draft invoice it creates. It's recorded as contract financing, not revenue.

## Performance-based payments

Add the events on the contract with their amounts and completion criteria (together at most 90% of the price). When one is done, select **Complete** and record the evidence, then **Bill completed events**.

## Deliveries pay financing back

Every FFP or T&M invoice on a financed contract gets a **Liquidation** line: the liquidation rate of the delivery, up to what is still outstanding. The contract page shows what has been billed, liquidated and is left.

## Send it to PIEE

On an approved invoice, the **PIEE (WAWF)** panel lists anything missing. When it's complete, select **Download PIEE file**, upload it in PIEE, then select **Mark submitted** with the PIEE number. Record **Accepted** or **Rejected** (with the reason) when PIEE answers. Nothing is sent to PIEE automatically.
