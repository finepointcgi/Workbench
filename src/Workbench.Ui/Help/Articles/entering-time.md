Your timesheet is a legal record. The government audits it ([DCAA](term:dcaa)), so a few rules matter more here than in most companies. They're simple once you know them.

## The rules, in plain words

1. **Enter your time every day.** Record each day's hours on the day you work them. Workbench notes when each day was first entered.
2. **Record every hour you work**, on the task you worked on, including time on company work like training or proposals ([indirect](term:indirect)) and leave.
3. **Only charge what you actually did.** Never move hours to a different project because it has more budget.
4. **Changes leave a trail.** Once you've submitted, any change asks for a reason and is kept in the history. That's normal, not a problem.
5. **You certify your own timesheet.** Nobody else may enter or submit it for you.

## Enter your hours

1. Open **Time** from the menu (or from the "Start your timesheet" item on your home page).
2. Each row is a [charge code](term:charge-code), shown as the task name followed by its code, for example "Requirements (1001.01.02)".
3. Type the hours in today's box. Use **Add row** and **Add a charge code** to add a task you're assigned to.
4. Select **Save**. Come back tomorrow and do the same.

If a task you need isn't in the list, you probably aren't assigned to it yet. Ask your project manager.

## Submit at the end of the period

1. Check every working day has hours, including holidays and leave on their leave codes.
2. Select **Certify and submit**.
3. Read the statement, tick **I certify this timesheet**, and select **Certify and submit** again.

If you're below your [standard hours](term:standard-hours), Workbench warns you. That's fine if it's true (part-time, unpaid leave), but make sure you haven't forgotten a day.

Your timesheet now says "Submitted, waiting on" your supervisor. When they approve it you'll get a notification. If they send it back, the banner says **Sent back for changes** with their comment: fix it and submit again.

> [!NOTE]
> Need to fix a day you already submitted? See [Correct a past day](help:correcting-time).
