Use an expense report to get paid back for money you spent on company or project work.

## Start a report

1. Open **Expenses** and select **Start an expense report** (or **New report**).
2. Give it a title, like "DC program review, Oct 5–7".

## Add your expenses

1. Select **Add line** for each expense: the date, what it was, the amount and the [charge code](term:charge-code) it belongs to.
2. Attach a receipt. Receipts are required at or above your company's limit (often $75), and always for lodging.
3. If something can't be billed to the government (for example alcohol), it's marked [unallowable](term:unallowable). It's still paid back if company policy allows, but never billed.

## Add per diem for travel

Per diem is a daily allowance for lodging and meals set by the GSA for each city.

1. In the report, select **Add per diem**, then **Add a destination** with the city and your dates.
2. Select **Save and compute**. Workbench works out the daily amounts, including the reduced meal rate on your first and last day.
3. Select **Update expense lines**. The per diem appears as ordinary lines on your report.

Per diem meals don't need receipts; lodging does.

## Submit

Select **Submit for approval**. Your home page reminds you if receipts are missing. The approver may send it back with a comment; fix it and submit again. You get a notification when it's approved.
